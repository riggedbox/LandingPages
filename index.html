[ 01 // ABOUT US ] Who We Are

Rigged Box Softworks is a game development studio based in Surakarta, Indonesia.

We craft immersive and innovative gaming experiences with a passion for storytelling and cutting-edge technology.

(a)

Concept

We start with a strong vision, blending creativity with market insights to craft unique gaming concepts. Our goal is to design experiences that resonate with players worldwide.

(b)

Development

Utilizing advanced game engines and tools, we bring our ideas to life with polished mechanics, stunning visuals, and engaging gameplay. Quality is at the core of our process.

(c)

Polish

Every detail matters. We refine animations, optimize performance, and ensure a seamless experience across all platforms, delivering games that captivate and entertain.

(d)

Launch

Our journey doesn’t end at release. We support our games post-launch with updates, community engagement, and ongoing improvements to enhance the player experience.

[ 02 // SERVICES ] What We Do

Our services are designed to empower your business in the digital age.

Brand Identity

We craft unique brand identities that resonate with your target audience and make a lasting impact. From logo design to brand guidelines, we ensure your brand stands out with a cohesive visual and narrative identity.

Product Design

We design user-centered products that are intuitive and functional. From wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes, our product design services ensure seamless user experiences that align with your business goals.

Frontend Development

We create responsive and visually appealing websites and applications. Our frontend development services bring your designs to life, ensuring smooth interactions and optimal performance across all devices.

UX Research

We conduct thorough user research to understand behaviors, needs, and challenges. Our UX research services help inform design decisions, ensuring that the products we create are both usable and valuable for your users.

E-Commerce

We offer comprehensive e-commerce solutions to help businesses reach their customers online. From platform setup and integration to custom payment systems, we ensure a seamless shopping experience that boosts your sales.

3D/2D Assets

We create high-quality 3D and 2D assets for games, websites, and applications. Our assets are optimized for performance, ensuring they enhance your digital experiences without compromising on quality.

Game Development

We specialize in game development, from concept to deployment. Whether you need a mobile game, desktop game, or VR experience, we work with you to create engaging and interactive experiences for your audience.
